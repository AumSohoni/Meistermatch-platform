General rules for LVS ISO 690:2023 (RTU adaptation):

- **Author**: Surname, initial(s). Format: Negnevitsky, M.
- **Title**: Italic, sentence case. Title proper in italics.
- **Edition, publisher, year, page count**: Included where applicable.
- **"Online" tag**: Must be added for any internet source, followed by the full access URL.
- **Hanging indent**: All lines after the first are indented (0.5 cm or equivalent).
- ** standards**: Include standard code, title, issuing organization, year, and page count if applicable.
- **Patents**: Same format as books, with "Online" and URL added for patents retrieved from Espacenet or similar.
- **Software**: Title (italic). Version. Developer, year. Available from: <URL>.
- **Legal acts**: Name of the act (italic), year, official gazette/source.
- **Unpublished works**: Indicate as "Unpublished" or "Manuscript".
